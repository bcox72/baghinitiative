Our vision

The Kingdom of God is increasing among Bengali Muslims as the Holy Spirit fuels Gospel engagement, ensuring every person knows a follower of Christ and is invited into relationship with Him.

Our mission

A five-year, collaborative effort uniting churches, mission agencies, and Kingdom-minded donors to see the Gospel spread deeply and broadly among the Bengali Muslim people.

It began in Phoenix in 2024.

How it began

When churches unite, heaven moves.

In 2024, several church and mission organization leaders based in Phoenix, Arizona began praying together with one shared burden: to reach those with the least access to the Gospel.

What began as a whisper of possibility is becoming a rallying cry—a movement designed to do together what no single church could accomplish alone.

“That they may all be one… so that the world may believe that you have sent me.”John 17:20–21

Why Bengali Muslims?

A vast people group. A profound opportunity.

More than 135 million Bengali Muslims live primarily in Bangladesh. The Bagh Initiative exists to strengthen and resource local Bengali believers as they share the hope of Jesus Christ with their own people.

Reaching this community requires cultural wisdom, collaborative ministry, generous investment, faithful perseverance, and—above all—sustained prayer.
